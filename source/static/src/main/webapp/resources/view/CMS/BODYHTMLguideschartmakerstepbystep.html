Chart Maker Online Step By Step

Last reviewed: 2026-07-30

Chart Maker Online runs in three steps: open the tool, provide your input, then copy the result. This page walks through what each step expects.


Step 1 - open the tool

Go to Chart Maker Online. Nothing needs to be installed or signed in before you start.


Step 2 - provide your input

Switch between chart types to find the clearest view of your numbers.


Step 3 - copy the result

Everything runs in the browser - no upload and no account.

For exporting or converting the underlying data, see the related developer tools.
